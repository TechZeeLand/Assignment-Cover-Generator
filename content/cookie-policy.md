## Summary

Visitors who do not sign in receive no cookies from {site}. Cookies are used only when you sign in, and for optional ads if you accept them.

## Essential cookies

- **`acg_session`** keeps you signed in after "Sign in with Google" and protects forms against forgery. Created only when you sign in, lasts up to 30 days, removed when you sign out.
- **Administrator session** on the `/admin` area, for the site owner only.

## Local storage (stays in your browser)

- Your light or dark theme choice.
- Your cookie choice (accepted or essential only), so we do not ask again.

Nothing in local storage is sent to a server.

## Advertising cookies (optional)

If the site owner has switched ads on, Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to show and measure ads. They load only after you choose **Accept all** in the banner. Choosing **Essential only** means no advertising cookies are set. Learn more in [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Change your choice

[Change cookie settings](#cookie-settings) at any time. If you had accepted advertising cookies and now choose Essential only, the page reloads without ad scripts.
